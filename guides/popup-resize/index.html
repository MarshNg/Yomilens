Popup resizing

Setup & usage

  1. 1

    Open Settings → General and enter the default popup width and height you prefer, then save.

  2. 2

    You can also drag the popup edge during review. YomiLens remembers the most recently resized dimensions.

  3. 3

    Use Reset size when you want to return to the add-on defaults.
